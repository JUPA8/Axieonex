WHAT THESE PREVIEWS DELIBERATELY AVOID

No neon, no glow, no gradient washes, no empty hero acreage, no low-contrast body text, no stock handshakes, no invented clients or results, and no animation that would cost you load time. Every number is a dash until you give me real data.
