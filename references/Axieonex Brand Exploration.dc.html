PHASE 01

Brand strategy

POSITIONING

Axieonex is the AI-orchestrated revenue engine that turns B2B outbound from a headcount problem into an engineered, measurable system.

PRIMARY AUDIENCE

Revenue decision-makers at B2B firms of 20–500 people — founders, CROs, VPs of Sales, Heads of Growth. Software, IT services, industrial tech, professional services. International, vendor-savvy, judging on predictability rather than novelty.

BRAND PROMISE

Predictable pipeline, engineered. AI does the volume, senior humans do the judgement, and you can see the mechanics.

PERSONALITY

The systems engineer of revenue: precise, calm, quietly confident, evidence-led. Speaks in mechanisms and numbers, never in hype.

EMOTIONAL RESPONSE

“These people are operators, not an agency — this will hold up in front of my board.”

FIVE CORE ATTRIBUTES
Orchestrated Precise Compounding Transparent Senior
CLICHÉS WE WILL NOT USE

Circuit textures, neural meshes, brains, robots, rising arrows, bar charts, hexagon grids, rockets, handshakes, lightning bolts, globes, cyan-on-black AI gradients, metallic 3D bevels.

VISUAL TERRITORY

Engineered restraint. Flat single-weight geometry on a visible construction grid; one ink, one paper, one disciplined accent; neutral grotesk with wide tracking; generous space. Closer to industrial instrumentation and financial infrastructure than consumer AI.

TAGLINE VERDICT — “AI-POWERED REVENUE ENGINE”

Keep revenue engine — it is a mechanism, it implies a system rather than a service, and it is ownable here. Drop AI-powered: it is a 2023 category tax, it dates the brand, and it puts the least differentiating word first. At avatar size the full line is unreadable, which is exactly why it currently damages the mark it is attached to.

Recommendation: refine it, and remove it from the primary logo. The tagline lives as typography beside the lockup in headers, decks and signatures — never locked into the mark. Every concept below is built to work with no tagline at all.

PHASE 02

The current mark, honestly

The idea is sound

A twin-peak abstract “A” with an implied axis is a legitimate, ownable structure. That geometry — and the words “revenue engine” — are the only assets worth carrying forward.

It is rendered, not designed

Bevels, metallic gradients, inner glow and a drop shadow are doing the work a shape should do. Remove the effects and there is very little silhouette left — the test any real mark has to pass.

It fails the small-size test

Hairline circuit strokes and node dots vanish below ~48 px; the hexagon core turns to mush at favicon size; the tagline is illegible in any avatar. Nothing survives embroidery, engraving or a fax-grade print.

It is in the wrong neighbourhood

Cyan-on-charcoal glow plus circuit rings reads gaming / crypto / cybersecurity — three categories your buyer explicitly discounts. It also cannot go monochrome, and the decorative backgrounds are not a brand system.

What carries forward

Only two things: the twin-apex structure (explored properly in Concept A) and the “engine” language. Everything else — the metal, the glow, the nodes, the hex core, the locked tagline — should go.

PHASE 03

Four directions

A
Twin Apex
REFINED EVOLUTION
#12161B · #C7643C · #F4F2ED
STRATEGIC IDEA

Keep the equity in the existing double-peak “A” and delete everything that was rendering rather than design. Same recognition, engineered properly.

MEANING

Two blades rise to unequal apexes — machine volume and human judgement — bound by one crossbar. Two forces, one axis, one letter.

CONSTRUCTION

Both blades are 11 units wide on a 64 grid and share one shear angle (26 units of run over 54 of rise). The right blade stops at 67% of the mark's height — its apex sits a third of the way down from the top — so the peak is deliberately asymmetric. The crossbar sits two units below true centre — optical, not mathematical — to keep the counter open.

WHY IT FITS

Zero recognition cost with the audience you already have, and it removes every attribute your buyer discounts. Lowest-risk path to a credible enterprise mark.

DISTINCTIVENESS

Almost every A-mark is symmetrical. This one leans, and the leaning apex is the memorable detail. Type: neutral grotesk semibold, tracking +3.5 (Söhne, Aeonik; Archivo as the free stand-in).

RISKS

A-monograms are a crowded field — chevron “A”s are everywhere in tech, and this silhouette also lives near architecture and construction brands. Its whole case rests on the asymmetry surviving at 16 px. Least distinctive of the four.

B
Convergence
REVENUE ORCHESTRATION
#101A2B · #0E9A8A · #F5F5F2
STRATEGIC IDEA

Many coordinated channels, one measurable output. The mark is a diagram of what you actually sell: orchestration, resolved into revenue.

MEANING

Two intake arms — AI volume and human expertise — converge on a single junction and leave as one solid bar. Pipeline in, revenue out, nothing lost in the middle.

CONSTRUCTION

Arms mirror around the horizontal axis and meet at 56% width; the output bar is 10 units deep on a 64 grid and exits the frame, so the mark is open-ended. The inner notch is a 6-unit V — wide enough to stay legible at 16 px.

WHY IT FITS

It explains the business in one shape to a buyer who has never heard of you — and the two-arms-one-output structure is a ready-made system for sub-brands (each service = one arm).

DISTINCTIVENESS

Directional without a single arrowhead, and the exiting bar gives it an unusual off-frame silhouette that stays recognisable in a rail or a tab. Type: geometric grotesk medium (Aeonik, GT Walsheim; Space Grotesk free).

RISKS

Can read as a funnel or a play button, and merge marks are common in logistics and payments. It must never be set with a gradient — one gradient and it becomes a generic fintech icon. Needs the wordmark more than the others do.

C
Compound
PRECISION & MOMENTUM
#0C0D10 · #D9A227 · #F6F4EF
STRATEGIC IDEA

Growth as compounding structure rather than a rising arrow. Momentum is expressed by shear, not by a pointer.

MEANING

Three stages of a pipeline, each taller than the last, every top edge leaning forward on the same angle. The heights are the compounding; the lean is the acceleration.

CONSTRUCTION

Columns are 14 units wide with 6-unit gaps on a 64 grid; leading-edge heights step 18 / 28 / 38 — a ~1.4× progression, optically corrected so the third column does not overpower. Every top edge rises exactly 7 units over its 14 units of width, so the three share one 26.6° shear and read as a single gesture rather than three bars.

WHY IT FITS

It says “measurable, staged, accelerating” to a numbers audience while staying abstract enough to sit on a SaaS product for a decade. The most enterprise-neutral of the four.

DISTINCTIVENESS

Three solid masses with no thin details — the strongest performer at 16 px and in embroidery, and the shear keeps it from reading as a chart. Type: grotesk medium, wide tracking (Founders Grotesk, Neue Haas; Archivo free).

RISKS

Closest of the four to something familiar: bar-chart and signal-strength iconography, and stepped-column marks exist in analytics and finance. The shear and the ratio are what separate it — lose them and it is generic.

D
Open Quadrant
WILDCARD
#191720 · #6E5DE7 · #F7F6F3
STRATEGIC IDEA

An axis system with one quadrant deliberately left open. The structure is what you build; the opening is what it is for.

MEANING

Three quarters of a rotated square: data, orchestration, human expertise. The missing lower-right quadrant is the exit — where revenue leaves the machine. Negative space carries the message.

CONSTRUCTION

A 64-unit square rotated 45° and split on both axes with a 4-unit gutter. Each quadrant is a right triangle whose hypotenuse is an original edge; the empty quadrant is exactly the size of a filled one, so the eye completes the diamond.

WHY IT FITS

It is a system, not just a mark: quadrants can carry sub-brand colours, become product icons, or rotate as a loading state — real range for a future SaaS platform.

DISTINCTIVENESS

The most unusual silhouette here and the only one that is instantly identifiable from its outline alone — an asymmetric diamond nobody else in this category owns. Type: grotesk medium (Roobert, Aeonik; Archivo free).

RISKS

Four-square and quadrant marks are common in consulting and BI; rotation and the single omission are the differentiators. It abandons all existing recognition, and the violet accent needs discipline or it drifts toward generic AI. Faintly reads as a compass — acceptable.

VECTOR EXECUTION

Every mark is pure path geometry: no embedded rasters, no filters, no gradients, no strokes thinner than 4 units, and no element that disappears when you flatten it to one colour. Files live in brand-exploration/concept-*/.

Wordmarks are live text for review only — outline the type before production so the lockup is font-independent.

No trademark availability is claimed for any name, mark or lockup shown here.
