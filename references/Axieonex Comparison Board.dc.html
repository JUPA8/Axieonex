MY PROFESSIONAL RECOMMENDATION

Develop C. Keep D on the table as the bolder option.

Why C. It is the most durable mark in the set: three solid masses, no thin details, best-in-set behaviour at 16 px and in one colour, and it says “measurable, staged, accelerating” to a numbers audience without a single chart or arrow cliché. Ink-on-bone with one gold accent also reads premium with no effects — which is exactly the failure mode we are correcting.

Why D stays in play. If the ambition is to look unlike anyone else in outbound, D is the more distinctive and the more extensible identity — quadrants become product icons, states and motion. It costs you all existing recognition and demands more discipline in application.

On A and B. Choose A only if continuity with the current mark genuinely matters commercially — it is the least distinctive option and inherits a crowded category. B is the best explanatory mark: pick it if the priority is a cold audience understanding the business from the logo alone.

Sanctioned combinations. If you want to blend, blend exactly two things: C's symbol with D's system thinking (stage columns as product icons), or C's geometry with B's navy-and-teal palette if gold feels too editorial. Nothing else — combining more would rebuild the problem we just removed.
