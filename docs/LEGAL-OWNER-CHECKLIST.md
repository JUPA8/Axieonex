# Legal owner and counsel confirmation checklist

Status: owner-blocked and not legally complete.

The public Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Cookies Policy are structural
drafts. The 22 placeholders below remain visible because the answers depend on
the operating entity, deployment, contracts, provider accounts, and legal
advice. Replacing them with generic wording would risk publishing false legal
claims. A qualified lawyer and the accountable business owner must supply and
approve the final language before these pages are relied on.

## Approval record required

For every answer, record the accountable owner, reviewing lawyer, approval
date, approved wording, affected page/version, and evidence supporting any
provider or security claim. Only then should the corresponding public
placeholder be replaced. Completion of this checklist is not itself legal
approval.
