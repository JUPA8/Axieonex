The operating sequence

Ten stages. One continuously running system.

This is not a one-time setup. Every stage keeps operating, feeding the next, and improving from what the last cycle learned.

01

Market definition

We define your ideal customer with you: industry, size, buying triggers.

02

Signal detection

AI continuously scans for funding, hiring and technology-stack signals that match your ICP.

03

Account prioritization

Accounts are scored and ranked by intent strength, not alphabetically or by list order.

04

Human validation

An analyst checks every high-priority account before it enters outreach.

05

Message strategy

Strategists define angle, tone and offer per segment, not one script for everyone.

06

Multi-channel execution

Email, LinkedIn and calling run as one coordinated cadence, not three separate efforts.

07

Response management

Every reply is read and handled by an experienced rep, including objections.

08

Qualification

Interest is confirmed against your definition of a qualified conversation before it moves forward.

09

Calendar handoff

The meeting lands directly on your calendar with full context on the account and signal.

10

Continuous optimization

Reply data and outcomes feed back into targeting and messaging for the next cycle.
